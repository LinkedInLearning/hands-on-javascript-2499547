Our Vision

To transform underserved neighborhoods into thriving, inclusive, and sustainable communities through thoughtful real estate investment.

Who We Are

We are a boutique real estate investment company driven by the belief that real estate should do more than generate financial returns—it should create opportunities, restore dignity, and strengthen communities. Our investment strategy focuses on identifying and acquiring vacant, abandoned, and neglected residential properties in underserved neighborhoods and transforming them into beautifully renovated, fully furnished, and professionally managed rental homes.

Our Approach

We believe neighborhood revitalization should benefit the people who already call these communities home. By prioritizing outreach to local residents in our rental strategy, we aim to preserve neighborhood identity, expand access to quality housing, and minimize displacement while bringing neglected properties back to productive use.

  1. 1 Community-Centered Investment

    Investing in overlooked neighborhoods with strong potential while prioritizing the needs of existing residents.

  2. 2 Property Revitalization

    Restoring abandoned and deteriorating homes through quality renovations, sustainable improvements, and thoughtful design.

  3. 3 Move-In-Ready Living

    Offering fully furnished, comfortable, and well-maintained homes that reduce the upfront burden of furnishing a household.

  4. 4 Local Economic Empowerment

    Engaging local contractors, tradespeople, and service providers so investment contributes directly to neighborhood economies.

  5. 5 Responsible Long-Term Ownership

    Building a sustainable rental portfolio that balances financial performance with affordability, community stability, and lasting neighborhood value.

Our Commitment

We measure success not only through property appreciation and rental income, but also through the positive impact our investments have on residents and neighborhoods. Our goal is to demonstrate that responsible real estate investment and meaningful community development can go hand in hand—creating attractive returns for investors while giving neglected homes a new purpose and helping existing communities flourish.

A Tenant’s Story

Four years ago, we purchased and renovated a house in an underserved community. During that time, we met N., who was working for his brother next door. He approached us with a vision: he wanted to rent the house and turn it into a group home.

At the time, N. had no established credit score or startup capital. What he did have was a strong understanding of the community, valuable connections, and the determination to build his business.

We believed in his potential and worked with him to get started. We furnished the house, signed a letter of intent, and allowed him to pay weekly, understanding that building a client base would take time.

Today, N. is meeting his full rental commitments, has been with us for three years, and is preparing to expand his business.

Watching his progress has been incredibly rewarding. His story reminds us that someone’s starting point does not define their potential. Sometimes, a little flexibility and the opportunity to get started can make a lasting difference.

Frequently asked questions

Where are your homes?

We’re based in Charlotte, North Carolina, and work in underserved communities across the Carolinas.

Who lives in your homes?

We lease our homes to working individuals and families, and to licensed group home providers who operate homes for their residents.

Are your homes furnished?

Yes. Our homes are fully furnished, comfortable, and well maintained, so you can move in without the upfront cost of furnishing a household.

How do I apply to rent a home?

Send us a message using the form below and choose “Renting a home”. We’ll let you know what’s available and walk you through our application.

What do providers need to lease a home?

We work with providers who are properly licensed or certified for their services. Contact us and we’ll walk you through our application.

Can I invest or partner with Akadelices?

We welcome conversations with investors, as well as brokers, contractors, lenders, and other partners who share our mission. Choose “Investing / partnering” in the contact form below.
